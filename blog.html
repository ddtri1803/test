How to treat hormonal acne naturally

Hormonal acne is driven by sebaceous activity and inflammatory signals, requiring structured dermatology care.

What is hormonal acne

Acne lesions around jawline/chin that often fluctuate with hormonal cycles.

Causes

Androgen shifts, stress, barrier damage, and comedogenic routines.

Skincare mistakes

Over-exfoliation, inconsistent SPF, and using strong actives without barrier support.

Dermatology treatment options

Retinoids, anti-inflammatory care, pigment control, and UV protection based on tolerance.
