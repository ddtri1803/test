Acne Treatment

What is Acne

Acne is an inflammatory condition of sebaceous follicles with lesions such as comedones, papules, and pustules.

Causes of Acne

Hormonal fluctuation, excess sebum, follicular plugging, bacterial overgrowth, and skin barrier disruption.

Types of Acne

Comedonal acne, inflammatory acne, and nodulocystic acne.

How We Treat Acne
